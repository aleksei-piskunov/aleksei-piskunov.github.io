## Research during PhD

My main focus has been around studying geometry of **moduli spaces of pure dimension one semistable sheaves** (also called relative compactified Jacobians) on **bielliptic surfaces**. They are the last (and least understood) piece of theory of moduli spaces of sheaves on surfaces of Kodaira dimension 0. My main results include computing the fundamental group, first and second Betti numbers, and proving that (for some types) Albanese fibers are Calabi–Yau varieties. Other results include computing some **weak Brill-Noether loci on Enriques surfaces** (ongoing collaboration project).
